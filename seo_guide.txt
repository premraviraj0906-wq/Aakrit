AAKRIT STUDIO — SEO ACTION GUIDE
===============================

Follow this step-by-step checklist to optimize and rank aakrit.online on search engines.

1. SUBMIT TO GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE
----------------------------------
To ensure Google indexes your site quickly:
- Go to Google Search Console (https://search.google.com/search-console/about)
- Sign in with your Google account.
- Add your property using "URL prefix": https://aakrit.online/
- In the left-hand menu, click on "Sitemaps".
- Under "Add a new sitemap", type the full URL: https://aakrit.online/sitemap.xml
- Click "Submit". Google will crawl and index your site over the next few days.

2. REGISTER A GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE
-------------------------------------
Essential for local SEO queries (e.g. "web developer near me"):
- Go to Google Business Profile (https://www.google.com/business/)
- Set up a profile for "Aakrit Studio" or "Aakrit".
- Choose categories like "Web Designer" or "Website Designer".
- Add your contact details, service area, and link to https://aakrit.online/
- Ask future clients to leave you 5-star reviews on Google to rank higher.

3. OPTIMIZE ON-PAGE CONTENT
---------------------------
- Keep your page speed fast. Since we built this using React + Vite on Vercel, it is already extremely fast.
- If you upload images in the future, compress them to WebP format before uploading to keep page loading times under 1 second.
- Target low-competition keywords in your headings and text, such as:
  * "Affordable custom React websites"
  * "Portfolio developer for creators"
  * "Bespoke frontend development studio"

4. BUILD BACKLINKS (OFF-PAGE SEO)
---------------------------------
Backlinks are "votes of confidence" that help Google trust your site:
- FOOTER LINKS: When you deliver a website to a client, include a small, clean attribution link in their footer:
  "Designed and Developed by Aakrit (https://aakrit.online)"
  This is the most powerful way for agency sites to gain authority.
- DIRECTORIES: Add your website link to your personal profiles on LinkedIn, GitHub, Behance, Dribbble, and Twitter.

5. MONITOR TRAFFIC & PERFORMANCE
--------------------------------
- Set up Vercel Analytics inside your Vercel project dashboard.
- Watch Google Search Console to see which keywords people use to find your site.

6. BRAND SCALING & MULTIMEDIA (GROWING THE BRAND)
-------------------------------------------------
To transition from a simple website to a highly trusted brand that commands premium rates, invest in these brand-growth pillars:

- CUSTOM BRAND LOGOS & IDENTITY:
  * Ensure a high-contrast, scalable vector logo (SVG) is used consistently across your website, favicons, invoices, and social media.
  * Define a strict, cohesive color palette (like Aakrit's electric blue and yellow) and custom typography to make the brand instantly recognizable.

- VIDEO MARKETING & EXPLAINERS:
  * Create a 60-second "Brand explainer video" showing off your website's responsiveness, 3D scenes, and speed.
  * Publish short-form dev walkthroughs (YouTube Shorts, Reels, TikToks) showing the design-to-code process. Video holds engagement 5x longer than static copy.

- PROFESSIONAL BRAND PHOTOGRAPHY:
  * Avoid generic stock images of tech offices and laptops.
  * Use high-quality, customized photography of yourself, your work setups, or bespoke brutalist renders. Original visuals build authentic authority.

- SOCIAL PROOF & LOGO STACKING:
  * Once you complete client projects, add a "Trusted By" client logo marquee to your site.
  * Ask clients for video testimonials showing their faces or screens; this adds 10x more trust than static text reviews.
